> You should all be asking way more questions. In other words, you should all be trying harder to actually understand what you’re hearing. Don’t trust that the person (or AI model) you’re talking to knows what they’re doing, even if you think they’re smarter than you. Nobody understands complex software products. If you have deep domain knowledge of any area of a codebase, you will routinely find yourself correcting powerful AI models and principal engineers.
