* If adobo doesn't have a sour component that you can discern, then it's not really Filipino adobo. It can still be delicious of course :-)
* And if you don't like sour then that's valid of course :-) I do still recommend trying one of those canonical Filipino dishes that lead with sour. Maybe you will like it :-)
* My favourite of the canoncial sour dishes is sinigang (tamarind souring is amazing) of course, I do like a very sour adobo though (most of my non Filipino friends don't like adobo or sinigang as sour as I like it!)
